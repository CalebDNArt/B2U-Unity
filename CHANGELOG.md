## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

### This is the first beta release of **B2U Material Master**.

There are too many features in the initial release to highlight them all. Major features include:
- **Added** - Ubershaders for high levels of batching.
- **Added** - Support for opaque, opaque double-sided, transparent, crystal, and liquid workflows.
- **Added** - Unity Editor tweaking for global profiles, individual palettes, and changing materials on meshes.
- **Added** - Volumetric refraction for liquids & crystals.
- **Added** - Fullscreen features for edge detection, scene recoloring, stylization, and status effects.
- **Added** - B2U RSUV component for per object shader control.
- **Added** - B2U Animation Trigger component for saving animation of B2U global floats, material properties/colors, RSUV values, fullscreen effects, and data swaps. Supports Unity Animator and UnityEvent triggering.
- Much more, please refer to the [DNArt Website](https://dnart.ca/b2u) for full documentation of features.
